# REQ-000 — Short requirement name

## Requirement

State observable behavior or constraint without prescribing implementation.

## Rationale

Why this matters to the user or business.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given/when/then or another testable outcome.

## Success measure

The number that shows this works for users (for example tasks completed per
week) and how it will be tracked. Write "none" only for purely technical work.

## Edge and failure behavior

- Validation, permissions, empty/loading/error/recovery behavior as relevant.

## Provenance

- Source: `source/artifact.ext`, section or locator
- Classification: provided | derived | inferred | unknown | pending | confirmed
- Confirmed by/date: Pending

## Dependencies and notes

- Related requirements, decisions, workflows, or constraints.
